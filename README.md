#SmartBudget Manager
An Excel workbook to record your monthly income and expenses, sort spending into categories, and see your balance and a budget warning on one dashboard.
File: SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx | Documentation version: 1.0

What it does:
1.Records income and expenses (date, description, type, category, amount, notes).
2.Calculates the monthly balance automatically (income − expenses).
3.Totals spending by category and compares it with your monthly budget.
2.Shows a pie chart of expenses by category.
3.Shows WITHIN BUDGET (green) or OVER BUDGET (orange/red) for your overall budget

What you need:
1.A computer with Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible program).
2.The file SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx.
3.No internet connection, no macros, no installation

What is inside:
Worksheet	Purpose
Dashboard	Read-only results: total income, total expenses, monthly balance, budget status, chart
Transactions	Where you type income and expenses (rows 4–50)
Categories	The 5 categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education) and their monthly budgets
Quick start
1.Double-click SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx. If you see Protected View, select Enable Editing.
2.Open Transactions. Delete the sample rows (select Date to Notes in rows 4–9, press Delete). Do not clear the Month column.
3.In the first empty row, enter the Date and Description.
4.Choose Income or Expense in Type, and pick a Category from the list (leave it empty for income).
5.Type the amount in the Income or Expense column, then press Enter.
6.Open Dashboard to see your totals, balance, chart and budget status.
7.To change a budget, edit Monthly Budget in Categories.
Good to know
Use one file per month: the Dashboard adds up all transactions in the file.
Only rows 4–50 are counted (47 transactions).
Do not type over the Month column or any Dashboard cell: they hold formulas.
Use the five existing categories; new category names are not shown on the Dashboard.
The warning applies to your overall budget. A category over its own budget shows a negative number in Remaining.
